Making South Asianleadership the norm.

SALUTE is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and private professional network for South Asian women and allies.

We build leadership infrastructure through trusted relationships, curated experiences, and reciprocal access. Give and Ask is how that infrastructure moves: members name what they can offer, what they need, and where they can open a door for someone else.
