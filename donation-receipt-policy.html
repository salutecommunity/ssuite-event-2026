This page applies to monetary gifts made through the Give page for the S.Suite event. For related materials, read the Event Terms & Conditions and the Event Privacy Notice.

Effective August 27, 2026 · SALUTE Foundation

We do not provide tax advice. A receipt records a verified payment and states the organization's tax status. Whether a specific contribution is deductible on your return depends on your circumstances. Please consult your own tax adviser or applicable guidance.

The receiving organization

Gifts made through the Give page are received by SALUTE Foundation, an organization recognized by the IRS as tax-exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Gifts here are benefit-free

A gift made through the Give page supports the organization. No goods or services are provided in exchange for it. A donation does not include admission, a ticket, a seat, a table, a guest place, an auction item, or any other event benefit, and it does not reserve or hold any of them.

If you want to attend the event, purchase a ticket or table through the Attend page. That is a separate transaction and is treated differently, as described below.

Your receipt

A receipt is issued only after Stripe verifies that your payment succeeded. Nothing on this site issues a receipt before that verification.

Your emailed receipt is sent to the address you enter at checkout, from S.Suite by SALUTE at ssuite@salute.community. The underlying record includes your name, the verified amount received, the payment date, a receipt number, and the payment transaction identifier held with the payment record.

If your receipt does not arrive, check your spam or filtered mail first, then write to us and we will re-send it.

Acknowledgment wording

Receipts for benefit-free gifts made through the Give page carry this acknowledgment:

SALUTE Foundation is a tax-exempt organization under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code (EIN 92-2955808). No goods or services were provided in exchange for this contribution. Your contribution may be tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. Please retain this receipt for your records.

Monthly gifts

If you choose a monthly gift, your first verified payment is acknowledged by email as described above. Each later monthly payment is recorded against your donation record when the payment succeeds.

We do not currently send a separate email for every subsequent monthly payment. Write to us at any time for documentation covering your monthly gifts, or to change or cancel them.

Employer matching

Many employers match charitable gifts. The Give page shows the organization details your employer's matching program is likely to ask for, and lets you copy them.

Matching is initiated by you through your employer's own matching portal. We do not submit a match request on your behalf, and we cannot confirm your eligibility or the amount your employer will match — those are determined entirely by your employer.

Refunds and corrections

If you gave in error, gave twice, entered the wrong amount, or need a name or email corrected on your record, write to us and we will review it. Refunds and record corrections for donations are handled by staff rather than automatically.

Where a gift is refunded, the receipt for that gift should not be relied on for tax purposes. Please retain our written confirmation of the refund instead.

Tickets and tables are different

Ticket and table purchases are not covered by this page. Purchasers receive a payment confirmation only.

We do not state a deductible amount, a charitable portion, or a fair-market value for a ticket or table purchase at this time. Do not treat a ticket or table confirmation as a charitable-contribution receipt.

Questions

For a question about a gift, a receipt, a correction, or matching, contact ssuite@salute.community.
