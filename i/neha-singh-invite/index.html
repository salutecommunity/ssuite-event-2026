Introducing the inaugural S.SUITE Chair

Indra Nooyi

Former Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, PepsiCo

It is our honor to announce Indra Nooyi as the inaugural recipient of The S.SUITE Chair — SALUTE’s lifetime achievement honor, recognizing extraordinary leadership, enduring legacy, and lasting impact.

As Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of PepsiCo, she led a Fortune 50 company with operations in more than 180 countries, and was the chief architect of Performance with Purpose — PepsiCo’s pledge to do what was right for the business by being responsive to the needs of the world around it. Through board and advisory roles spanning business, healthcare, education, public service, and the arts, she continues to shape leading institutions.

Her impact has been recognized globally: 15 honorary degrees, India’s Padma Bhushan, the country’s third-highest civilian honor, inclusion in the Smithsonian National Portrait Gallery, and induction into the National Women’s Hall of Fame. Her New York Times bestselling memoir, My Life in Full: Work, Family and Our Future, reflects on the many roles that have shaped her life — as a leader, wife, mother, and daughter — while offering a call to action on how society can better support women.

Her life and career embody what The S.SUITE Chair was created to recognize: achievement at the highest level, and an enduring commitment to creating greater possibility for others.

In conversation with

Anu Aiyengar

Global Chair of Investment Banking, J.P. Morgan

A leading figure in global finance and previously the firm’s Global Head of M&A and Advisory, Anu has been named to Barron’s 100 Most Influential Women in Finance and was the first woman to rank No. 1 on Business Insider’s Rainmakers list. Her longstanding relationship with Indra will bring a distinct perspective to their keynote fireside conversation, spanning Indra’s career and the legacy she continues to build.

SALUTE

South Asian Leaders Unite to Empower.

SALUTE is a private, professional network dedicated to creating impactful connections between South Asian women.

Our mission is to expand access, visibility, and connection for South Asian women and allies. Our members are executives, trailblazers, and pioneers who are shaping the present and influencing the future.

SALUTE Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.

S.SUITE

You can’t celebrate what you can’t see.

South Asian women have always been in the room. What’s missing is the spotlight. Recognition is how the narrative shifts — when we name greatness out loud, we make it possible for the next generation to imagine themselves there too. The S.SUITE exists to make that visible.

Honorees across twelve categories, including two Ally honors, will form the inaugural S.SUITE. The nomination pool also powers SALUTE’s category lists — curated public spotlights featuring standout leaders beyond the final honorees.

Celebrating excellence

Nominees are leaders whose work deserves to be seen — and whose influence helps move others forward. Honorees will be selected across these twelve categories, including two Ally honors, to form The S.SUITE.

The Chair

A lifetime recognition for a South Asian woman whose career over decades has reshaped ceilings, opened paths, and expanded what leadership can look like for our community.

CEO

A sitting or recent CEO whose leadership has materially shifted an organization, sector, or market — and who uses that role to elevate other South Asian women into senior seats.

Director

A board director or senior governance leader shaping strategy, oversight, and accountability in the boardroom, while advocating for more South Asian women in governance roles.

Operator

An operator — COO, CFO, CHRO, product, strategy, General Counsel, or equivalent — whose decisions and execution have transformed how an organization actually runs and delivers.

Builder

A founder, co-founder, or senior architect of a company, fund, platform, practice, or initiative, whose work creates new opportunities or shifts the landscape for others.

Capital

A leader in capital, markets, investing, or dealmaking whose decisions move capital and create access for operators and founders.

Culture

A leader working in or through culture — arts, media, brand, content, policy, or public platforms — whose work has shifted how South Asians are seen and whose stories are centered.

Impact

A leader whose work drives meaningful impact for the broader South Asian community, through community-building, philanthropy, ecosystem design, talent pipelines, or social impact.

Rising

A rising leader on a clear path to the C-suite or boardroom, already operating with significant scope and lifting others as she advances.

Civic & Legal

A leader in law, public service, policy, or civic institutions whose work strengthens justice, accountability, and participation, and expands access, representation, or opportunity for communities.

Health

A leader in healthcare, medicine, life sciences, or public health whose work improves how people live and receive care, from clinical leadership and research to health-system transformation.

Ally · Two honors

Two honors: one for a South Asian man and one for a non–South Asian leader, each with a sustained, concrete track record of allyship — backing South Asian women into meaningful roles, boards, deals, and platforms.
