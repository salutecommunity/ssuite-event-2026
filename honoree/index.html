About the evening

S.SUITE is the inaugural honors evening of SALUTE (South Asian Leaders Unite to Empower), a 501(c)(3) private professional network expanding access, visibility and connection for South Asian women leaders and allies.

The evening includes a seated dinner and the honors presentation, followed by a fireside conversation with Indra Nooyi, recipient of The S.SUITE Chair, moderated by Anu Aiyengar, Global Chair of Investment Banking at J.P. Morgan.

Please note

To accept, honorees must attend in person on Friday, November 20, 2026, at 6:30 p.m. at the Prince George Ballroom in New York City. Accepting does not require any purchase, sponsorship or donation.
