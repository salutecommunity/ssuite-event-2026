Inviting someone to your table

Dear [Name],

We would be delighted to welcome you to our table at the inaugural S.SUITE Honors Ceremony on Friday, November 20, 2026, at the Prince George Ballroom in New York City.

SALUTE is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and private professional network for South Asian women and allies, building connections and supporting leadership across industries. Its vision is to make South Asian leadership the norm.

S.SUITE is SALUTE’s honors program, recognizing South Asian women leaders and allies whose work is shaping business, capital, culture, and community. The inaugural ceremony brings that community together for an evening of recognition and connection.

The evening begins at 6:30 p.m. and includes a cocktail reception, dinner, and the honors program. Indra Nooyi will receive The S.SUITE Chair, SALUTE’s lifetime recognition, followed by a fireside conversation moderated by Anu Aiyengar, Global Chair of Investment Banking at J.P. Morgan.

As an S.SUITE partner, we are hosting a table and would love for you to join us. Your seat is included at our table; no ticket purchase is required.

Please let us know if you can attend. We will then send you a private registration link to confirm your details.

About SALUTE and S.SUITE

SALUTE is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and private professional network for South Asian women and allies, building connections and supporting leadership across industries. Its vision is to make South Asian leadership the norm.

S.SUITE is SALUTE’s honors program, recognizing South Asian women leaders and allies whose work is shaping business, capital, culture, and community. Its inaugural Honors Ceremony takes place on November 20, 2026, in New York City.
