Inviting someone to your table

Dear [Name],

I’d love for you to join [Company] at our table for SALUTE’s inaugural S.SUITE Honors Ceremony on Friday, November 20, 2026, at the Prince George Ballroom in New York City.

SALUTE is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and private, professional network that expands access, visibility, and connection for South Asian women leaders and allies. S.SUITE is SALUTE’s honors platform, recognizing leaders across business, capital, and culture. Each year’s ten honorees form The S.SUITE—a leadership team that works together to advance a community initiative.

This year’s program recognizes Indra Nooyi with The S.SUITE Chair, SALUTE’s lifetime recognition, followed by a fireside conversation moderated by Anu Aiyengar, Global Chair of Investment Banking at J.P. Morgan.
