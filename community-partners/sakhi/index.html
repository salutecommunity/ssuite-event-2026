Guests access this special rate through your dedicated invitation page and register and pay directly. The nine-ticket allowance applies to your organization’s guests in total, across all purchases—not nine tickets per guest.

Introducing the inaugural S.Suite Chair

Indra Nooyi

Former Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, PepsiCo

It is our honor to announce Indra Nooyi as the inaugural recipient of The S.Suite Chair — SALUTE’s lifetime achievement honor, recognizing extraordinary leadership, enduring legacy, and lasting impact.

As Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of PepsiCo, she led a Fortune 50 company with operations in more than 180 countries, and was the chief architect of Performance with Purpose — PepsiCo’s pledge to do what was right for the business by being responsive to the needs of the world around it. Through board and advisory roles spanning business, healthcare, education, public service, and the arts, she continues to shape leading institutions.

Her impact has been recognized globally: 15 honorary degrees, India’s Padma Bhushan, the country’s third-highest civilian honor, inclusion in the Smithsonian National Portrait Gallery, and induction into the National Women’s Hall of Fame. Her New York Times bestselling memoir, My Life in Full: Work, Family and Our Future, reflects on the many roles that have shaped her life — as a leader, wife, mother, and daughter — while offering a call to action on how society can better support women.

Her life and career embody what The S.Suite Chair was created to recognize: achievement at the highest level, and an enduring commitment to creating greater possibility for others.

In conversation with

Anu Aiyengar

Global Chair of Investment Banking, J.P. Morgan

A leading figure in global finance and previously the firm’s Global Head of M&A and Advisory, Anu has been named to Barron’s 100 Most Influential Women in Finance and was the first woman to rank No. 1 on Business Insider’s Rainmakers list. Her longstanding relationship with Indra will bring a distinct perspective to their keynote fireside conversation, spanning Indra’s career and the legacy she continues to build.

SALUTE

South Asian Leaders Unite to Empower.

SALUTE is a private, professional network dedicated to creating impactful connections between South Asian women.

Our mission is to expand access, visibility, and connection for South Asian women and allies. Our members are executives, trailblazers, and pioneers who are shaping the present and influencing the future.

SALUTE Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization.

S.Suite

You can’t celebrate what you can’t see.

South Asian women have always been in the room. What’s missing is the spotlight. Recognition is how the narrative shifts — when we name greatness out loud, we make it possible for the next generation to imagine themselves there too. The S.Suite exists to make that visible.

Ten honorees will be selected to form the inaugural S.Suite. The nomination pool also powers SALUTE’s category lists — curated public spotlights featuring standout leaders beyond the final honorees.
