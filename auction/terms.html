Silent Auction · S.Suite 2026

Auction terms.

These terms apply to online bidding in the S.Suite 2026 silent auction, run by SALUTE ("we", "S.Suite"). By registering to bid you agree to them.

1. Registering to bid

To bid you verify your email address with a one-time code, give your full name (and optionally a phone number), accept these terms, and save a payment card with our payment processor, Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. Saving a card does not charge it.

Your name, email and phone are visible only to authorised S.Suite staff. Other bidders see only your bidder number.

2. Bids are binding

Every bid is an offer to buy the lot at that amount if it is the winning bid. Bids are in whole US dollars and must be at least the opening bid, or the current high bid plus the minimum increment. Bids cannot be withdrawn. We may ask you to confirm unusually large bids, and we may decline bids above a lot's maximum to catch typing mistakes.

3. Bidding window and soft close

Bidding opens and closes at the times shown on the auction pages (Eastern Time). If a bid is placed in the last 2 minutes of a lot, that lot's close is extended by 2 minutes, so each lot ends only after 2 minutes without a new bid. Our server clock is the official time. We may pause, extend or close bidding, or withdraw a lot, at any time, including for technical problems.

4. Winning and payment

When a lot closes, the highest valid bid is the provisional winner. S.Suite staff review each result and then charge the winning amount to the card you saved. We will email you before and after the charge. If the charge fails, we will contact you; if we cannot collect payment, we may offer the lot to the next-highest bidder at their highest bid.

5. All sales final

All sales are final. There are no refunds, exchanges or cash value, except where a donor cannot provide the lot, in which case we will refund the amount you paid or, with your agreement, offer a comparable substitute.

6. Fulfilment by the donor

Lots are donated and provided by their donors, not by SALUTE. Experiences are scheduled directly with the donor on a mutually agreed date, subject to availability, and must be used within the period stated on the lot (or within 12 months if none is stated). Descriptions, photos and values are provided by donors in good faith; lots are offered as described, without other warranties.

7. Values and taxes

Each lot shows a fair market value provided by the donor. Your receipt will show the amount you paid and that fair market value. We do not give tax advice: whether any part of your payment is deductible depends on your circumstances and applicable law. Please consult your own tax adviser.

8. Conduct

One registration per person. We may cancel registrations or bids we believe are fraudulent, abusive or placed in error, and may decline to accept a bidder at our discretion.

9. Contact

Questions: ssuite@salute.community. Our privacy notice explains how we handle personal information.
