Founder & CEO

Pooja Bavishi

Pooja Bavishi is the founder and CEO of Malai and the author of Malai: Frozen Desserts Inspired by South Asian Flavors. Trained in urban planning and public policy, she was always the one baking for coworkers and celebrations. In her final semester of business school she made ice cream with Indian spices for the first time, and the idea for Malai was born.

“You cannot get more American than ice cream, and these flavors were so ubiquitous to my upbringing.”

She founded Malai in Brooklyn in 2015 and has grown it into a multi-market brand. Pooja and Malai have been featured in The New York Times, Bon Appétit, Barron’s and Forbes.

The Brand

Malai

Founded in Brooklyn in 2015 by Pooja Bavishi, Malai is a leading ice cream brand redefining American ice cream through bold, Indian-inspired flavors. Rooted in Pooja’s first-generation Indian-American upbringing, Malai brings South Asian ingredients and culinary traditions into a new expression of American ice cream, pairing unexpected flavors with exceptionally creamy textures.

Malai’s treats are always eggless, with select vegan offerings, and include ice cream, kulfi pops, ice cream cakes and ice cream sandwiches.

With scoop shops in New York City, Washington, DC and Philadelphia, nationwide shipping, and distribution in select grocery stores across the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic, Malai is bringing its distinctive approach to ice cream to an expanding audience.
