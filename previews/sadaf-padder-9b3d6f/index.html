The experience

Enjoy a private, curator-led gallery walk through New York City’s Chelsea gallery district with independent curator and art advisor Sadaf Padder.

Designed for up to 10 guests, this experience includes visits to five to six carefully selected gallery exhibitions, offering an insider’s perspective on contemporary art and the New York art market.

The itinerary will be tailored to current exhibitions and the group’s interests, with time for conversation and questions along the way. It’s ideal for new and seasoned collectors alike, or simply for anyone who has wondered what goes on behind Chelsea’s gallery doors.

Curator & Art Advisor

Sadaf Padder

Sadaf Padder is a Brooklyn-based curator, writer, educator and art advisor whose work centers artists from South Asian, African and other diasporic communities. She has curated exhibitions and programs across the country, from Philadelphia and New York to Los Angeles and Martha’s Vineyard, exploring cultural memory, Islamic visual cultures and the ways contemporary artists revisit historical traditions to envision alternative futures.

“Belonging is something assembled through stories, objects, and memory rather than a fixed place.”

Her recent curatorial projects include Bounty at Sargent’s Daughters and Mad Heart, Be Brave at Fridman Gallery. Her writing has appeared in Artsy, Hyperallergic, ARTnews and Cultured, and she was a 2022–23 Hyperallergic Arts Writing Fellow.

Art That Gives Back

The advisor’s eye

As an art advisor, Sadaf has facilitated acquisitions for institutions including the RISD Museum and the Baltimore Museum of Art, and helps collectors build thoughtful collections rooted in relationships with artists.

She is the founder of Alpha Arts Alliance and serves on the boards of the Vera List Center for Art and Politics, Twelve Gates Arts and Grown in Haiti.
