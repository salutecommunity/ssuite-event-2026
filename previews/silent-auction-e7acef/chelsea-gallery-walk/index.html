The experience

Enjoy a private, curator-led gallery walk through New York City’s Chelsea gallery district with independent curator and art advisor Sadaf Padder.

Designed for up to 10 guests, this experience includes visits to five to six carefully selected gallery exhibitions, offering an insider’s perspective on contemporary art and the New York art market.

The itinerary will be tailored to current exhibitions and the group’s interests, with time for conversation and questions along the way. It’s ideal for new and seasoned collectors alike, or simply for anyone who has wondered what goes on behind Chelsea’s gallery doors.

Curator & Art Advisor

Sadaf Padder

Sadaf Padder is a Brooklyn-based curator, writer, and art advisor whose work centers artists of the global majority, with a particular focus on the South Asian diaspora. She is the founder of Alpha Arts Alliance, a curator-led art advisory connecting collectors with contemporary artists and their practices.

She has curated exhibitions and programs across the country, from Philadelphia and New York to Los Angeles and Martha’s Vineyard. Her curatorial practice explores cultural memory, ecology, and the ways artists draw upon inherited histories and traditions to imagine alternative futures.

“Belonging isn't always tied to a place. Sometimes it's found in the stories we inherit, the things we hold onto, and what we choose to create.”

Her recent curatorial projects include BOUNTY at Sargent’s Daughters and Mad Heart, Be Brave at Fridman Gallery. She has facilitated the acquisition of artworks by contemporary artists for institutions including the Baltimore Museum of Art, RISD Museum, Northwestern University, and the Nion McEvoy Foundation for the Arts.

Her writing has appeared in Artsy, Hyperallergic, ARTnews, and Cultured. She was a 2022–23 Emily J. Hall Tremaine Fellow at Hyperallergic, where she developed a project exploring South Asian futurisms. She serves on the boards of Twelve Gates Arts and the Vera List Center for Art and Politics and is a visiting art critic at Columbia University.
