The piece

A limited-edition, solar-powered watch inspired by the energy of New York, from VIANI Watch Company.

Bold blue meets orange in VIANI’s New York Solar, a small-batch edition created as a tribute to the city where the brand began. A polished stainless steel case and bracelet give its celebratory palette an everyday elegance.

Powered by indoor and outdoor light, the watch pairs a solar movement with sapphire crystal and a distinctive Devanagari date display—a subtle expression of South Asian heritage, carried on the wrist.

The designer

The founder & the brand.

Founder

Sunny Bhathela

Sunny Bhathela founded VIANI Watch Company in New York City in 2023 after searching for modern Indian watches and finding few that reflected his vision. He set out to create timepieces that celebrate South Asian design for a global audience.

His interest in Indian numerals and their history helped shape VIANI’s design language. The brand brings that cultural perspective to contemporary watchmaking, working with suppliers across South Asia, Switzerland and Japan.

The Brand

VIANI Watch Company

VIANI is a South Asian-owned watch brand that brings South Asian design and ideas into contemporary watchmaking. Founded in New York City, it creates timepieces that combine cultural detail with everyday wearability.

The limited-edition New York Solar pairs a blue sun-ray dial, orange accents and a Devanagari date display with a solar-powered movement, sapphire crystal and stainless steel bracelet.
